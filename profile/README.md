Felag is a boutique engineering studio whose principal-led pods design and ship production systems for funded fintech and technology companies, from backend and infrastructure at scale to payments and identity, with every engagement priced on the outcome.

We take on the parts of a product where a mistake costs money or a customer. Teams usually bring us in when an integration, a launch or a bill has outgrown the backend and there is no senior hire in time. We do not staff juniors or rotate engineers; a named principal owns the architecture from the first sprint to production.

### What we build

- High-load backends: high-throughput services, API and LLM gateways, rate limiting, routing, caching, and cloud and inference cost.
- Money movement: multi-currency payouts, split payments, usage-based billing, high-precision ledgers, settlement and reconciliation.
- Enterprise access: multi-tenant isolation, granular authorization, SSO, SCIM, tamper-resistant audit logs and data residency.
- Distributed ledger systems where the product calls for them: node and RPC gateways, rollups, staking infrastructure and smart contracts.

### Proven in production

- We delivered the platform behind a non-custodial debit card programme end to end: an L2 chain that launched in August 2024, and the application layer, indexer, oracles and account abstraction on top of it, built for MiCA alignment.
- We designed the RPC proxy that served 140k+ requests per second in production for a major browser wallet and a leading DEX aggregator.
- We designed a push oracle that delivered multi-asset prices every 500 ms and a pull-based feed at about 1,000 requests per second.

Every build starts with a short paid Architecture Sprint, then a principal-led pod priced on outcomes.
